**Portfolio Project | Shabiba Shahjahan, Business Analyst**

## Overview

This portfolio project demonstrates applied Business Analysis methodology for a Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations (D365 F&O) implementation. It simulates a real-world BA engagement for a fictional mid-size Canadian energy company (**NorthPeak Energy Corp.**) migrating from a legacy ERP to D365 F&O, focusing on the **Accounts Payable and Vendor Management** workstream.

The project was developed to demonstrate D365 F&O BA competency including requirements elicitation, user story development, gap analysis, process redesign, and KPI success measurement — skills directly applicable to ERP implementation BA roles.

## Business Context

**NorthPeak Energy Corp.** is migrating from a legacy ERP to D365 F&O. The current Accounts Payable process relies on:
- Manual 3-way invoice matching in Excel (2–3 day cycle time)
- Email-based approval chains with no audit trail
- Dual-system vendor onboarding creating data inconsistencies
- Monthly static aging reports with no real-time visibility

The D365 F&O implementation targets:
- 75% reduction in invoice processing time (8.5 → 2.1 days)
- 94% automated invoice match rate
- Full digital approval workflow with audit trail
- Real-time Power BI AP dashboard

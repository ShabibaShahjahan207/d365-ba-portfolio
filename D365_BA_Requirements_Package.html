Business Scenario

NorthPeak Energy Corp. is a mid-size Canadian energy company migrating from a legacy ERP to Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations. This requirements package covers the Accounts Payable and Vendor Management workstream — specifically the invoice processing, vendor onboarding, and payment approval workflows. The current state relies on manual data entry, disconnected spreadsheets, and email-based approval chains that create reconciliation errors and payment delays. The D365 F&O implementation will automate invoice matching, centralize vendor records, and introduce a structured digital approval workflow.

BA Note — Methodology This requirements package was developed following requirements elicitation sessions with the AP Manager, Finance Controller, and Procurement Lead. Requirements are documented using Agile user story format with INVEST-compliant acceptance criteria. All process flows represent current state validated by process walkthroughs and are mapped against D365 F&O standard capabilities to identify configuration and customization requirements.
User Stories & Acceptance Criteria
5 user stories covering AP invoice processing, vendor onboarding, and payment approval workflows
US-001 Automated 3-Way Invoice Matching HIGH ▼
"As an Accounts Payable Clerk, I want D365 F&O to automatically match vendor invoices against purchase orders and goods receipts, so that I can eliminate manual matching errors and reduce invoice processing time."
Acceptance Criteria
  • ✓System automatically matches invoice to PO and GR within 30 seconds of invoice entry
  • ✓Tolerances of ±2% on unit price and ±1 unit on quantity are configurable by finance admin
  • ✓Unmatched invoices are automatically flagged and routed to AP supervisor queue with mismatch details
  • ✓Matched invoices proceed to payment approval workflow without manual intervention
  • ✓All matching decisions are logged with timestamp and user ID for audit trail
US-002 Vendor Onboarding & Master Data Management HIGH ▼
"As a Procurement Officer, I want to onboard new vendors directly in D365 F&O with required documentation and approval workflows, so that vendor master data is accurate, complete, and audit-ready from day one."
Acceptance Criteria
  • ✓Vendor onboarding form captures: legal name, business number, banking details, payment terms, and tax classification
  • ✓System requires upload of W8/W9 or TD1 form before vendor record can be activated
  • ✓New vendor requests trigger a two-level approval workflow (Procurement Lead → Finance Controller)
  • ✓Duplicate vendor detection alerts users when a new vendor matches existing name, BN, or banking details
  • ✓Vendors are marked Active/Inactive rather than deleted to preserve historical records
US-003 Digital Payment Approval Workflow HIGH ▼
"As a Finance Controller, I want payment batches to route through a configurable digital approval chain in D365 F&O, so that all disbursements are authorized before processing and the approval trail is fully auditable."
Acceptance Criteria
  • ✓Payment batches under $10K require single approval (AP Manager); $10K–$100K require two approvals; $100K+ require CFO sign-off
  • ✓Approvers receive email notification with payment summary and one-click approve/reject from notification
  • ✓Rejected batches return to AP queue with mandatory rejection reason
  • ✓Approval SLA of 48 business hours triggers escalation notification to next level approver
  • ✓Complete approval chain history is visible on each payment batch record
US-004 AP Aging & Reporting Dashboard MEDIUM ▼
"As a Finance Manager, I want a real-time AP aging report and vendor payment dashboard in D365 F&O, so that I can monitor outstanding liabilities, identify overdue invoices, and make timely payment decisions."
Acceptance Criteria
  • ✓AP aging report displays outstanding invoices bucketed by: Current, 1–30 days, 31–60 days, 61–90 days, 90+ days
  • ✓Dashboard refreshes in real-time and filters by vendor, cost centre, and entity
  • ✓Report is exportable to Excel and PDF with one click
  • ✓Early payment discount opportunities are flagged for invoices with net-10 or net-15 terms
  • ✓Dashboard is accessible to Finance Manager and above without IT assistance
US-005 Vendor Self-Service Portal Integration MEDIUM ▼
"As a Vendor, I want to submit invoices and check payment status through a self-service portal connected to D365 F&O, so that I can reduce phone inquiries to NorthPeak's AP team and have visibility into my payment timeline."
Acceptance Criteria
  • ✓Vendors can log in via secure portal and submit invoices with PO reference number
  • ✓Portal displays real-time invoice status: Received, Under Review, Approved, Scheduled for Payment, Paid
  • ✓Vendors receive automated email confirmation within 1 hour of invoice submission
  • ✓Portal rejects invoices missing required fields with specific error messages
  • ✓Vendor portal data syncs to D365 F&O vendor master in real time via API integration

BA Note — Process Redesign Principles The future state process eliminates all manual handoffs between steps 1–4, reducing invoice processing time from an average of 8.5 business days to a target of 2 business days. Exception handling (unmatched invoices, rejected approvals) is routed through structured queues rather than email. All process changes have been validated against D365 F&O standard capabilities to minimize customization requirements.
